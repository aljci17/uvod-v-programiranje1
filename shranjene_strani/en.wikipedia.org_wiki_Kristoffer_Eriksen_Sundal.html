Kristoffer Eriksen Sundal (born 7 February 2001)[1] is a ski jumper from Norway.[2] At the 2026 Winter Olympics, he won a silver medal in the mixed team and a bronze medal in the men's super team.

Career

[edit]

Eriksen Sundal's debut in the FIS Ski Jumping World Cup took place in November 2022 in Wisła. As of 2025[update], he has 8 podiums in the World Cup, including four individual podiums and a win with the Norwegian team. He also finished 2nd at the Men's team large hill event at the FIS Nordic World Ski Championships 2023.[1]

Following the disqualification of Norwegian ski jumpers Johann André Forfang and Marius Lindvik at the men's large hill event due to ski-jumping suits manipulations, Robin Pedersen, Kristoffer Eriksen Sundal, and Robert Johansson also got temporary suspensions from competitions from FIS.[3]
