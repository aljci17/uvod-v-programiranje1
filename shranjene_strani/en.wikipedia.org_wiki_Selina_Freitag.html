Selina Alma Freitag (born 19 May 2001) is a German ski jumper.[3] She won the overall ranking of the 2016–17 FIS Ski Jumping Alpen Cup, and has competed at World Cup level since the 2018–19 season. Her best individual result is second place, achieved on eleven occasions.[4] At the 2019 Nordic Junior World Ski Championships, she won a silver medal in the team event and a bronze medal in the mixed team event.

Her brother Richard Freitag is also a former ski jumper.[5]
