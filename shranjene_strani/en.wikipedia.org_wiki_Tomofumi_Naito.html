Tomofumi Naito (内藤智文, Naito Tomofumi; born 22 February 1993 in Chōfu) is a Japanese ski jumper.

Career

[edit]

Naito represented Japan at the FIS Ski Flying World Championships 2026 and won a gold medal in the team event.[1] This was Japan's first medal in a FIS Ski Flying World Championships team event.[2]
