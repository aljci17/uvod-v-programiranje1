Urša Križnar (née Bogataj; born 7 March 1995) is a Slovenian former ski jumper.[2] She was the 2022 Olympic champion in the women's normal hill individual event.[3]

Bogataj injured her knee in 2022 and stopped competing, and then officially retired in February 2025.[4]
